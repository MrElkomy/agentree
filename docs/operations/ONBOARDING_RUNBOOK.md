# Assisted setup runbook (operator)

For an Agentree team member helping a new business go live. The customer does every step that
involves their credentials; the operator never asks for, sees or stores a customer's AI key or
password.

## Before the call
- [ ] The customer has read the terms and privacy notice (links on the sign-up page).
- [ ] The customer has provider accounts with billing (see `CUSTOMER_KEY_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Use synthetic test messages during setup; no real customer data in screenshots.

## During the call (screen share; the customer clicks)
1. **Sign up** with their work email, confirm the email, sign in.
2. **Create the business** (name, URL name).
3. **AI keys:** primary and backup from different providers, test on; set a provider spending
   limit. Confirm both show "Working" after the first chat.
4. **Knowledge:** add 5–10 entries (prices, opening hours, delivery, returns, contact). Publish.
   Optionally upload a PDF/DOCX and review the drafts before publishing.
5. **AI team:** name the Sales Agent; turn on Customer Service / Technical Support if useful;
   Data Analyst and Executive Assistant are internal (team only).
6. **Business hours** and what agents do outside them; a pause message for holidays.
7. **Test chat:** ask 3 real questions; fix knowledge where answers are weak.
8. **Public page & widget:** display name, bio, links; daily reply limit; allowed websites for the
   widget; copy the one-line widget snippet to their website.
9. **Team:** invite colleagues with the right role (Member / Admin).
10. **Alerts:** turn on push notifications on the owner's phone; choose daily or weekly email
    reports.
11. **Approvals:** explain "approve once" vs "always allow"; follow-up emails stay opt-in.

## After the call
- [ ] Send the customer `CUSTOMER_KEY_GUIDE.md` and the support contact.
- [ ] Check the next day that the first visitor conversations were answered (customer's Analytics).
- [ ] Nothing about the customer (keys, passwords, conversation content) is written in our notes.
